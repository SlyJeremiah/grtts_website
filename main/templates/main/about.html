About us

We train rangers, then we stand behind them in the field

GRTTS is a Zimbabwean ranger training, deployment and management company. We do not only run courses — we place rangers on the ground and keep managing them, so the people who protect your land answer to a standard someone is accountable for.

Who we are

GRTTS is a private ranger training, deployment and management company. We have trained over {{ site_facts.rangers_trained }} rangers to date and we cover {{ site_facts.hectares_protected }} hectares of private and community-owned wildlife area across Zimbabwe.

Our history

GRTTS was founded in {{ site_facts.founded_year }} by Zimbabwean conservationists and wildlife managers, and formally registered in Zimbabwe in {{ site_facts.registered_year }}. We have been active in wildlife protection for {{ site_facts.years_operating }} years.

Our mission

To empower men and women to serve on the frontlines of wildlife and environmental protection through professional training and deployment.

How we work

Our instructors have worked the ground they teach. Training is practical and field-based rather than classroom-led, and it continues after deployment — rangers we place remain under GRTTS management, with supervision, refresher training and reporting back to the landowner or NGO we work for.

What we offer

  • Ranger training
    Basic, advanced, tracking and specialist courses.
  • Ranger deployment
    Vetted, trained rangers placed on your property.
  • Ongoing management
    Supervision, patrol reporting and refresher training.
