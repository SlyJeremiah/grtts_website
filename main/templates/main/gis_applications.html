Why it matters for your property

A patrol plan drawn on a map covers ground; a patrol plan drawn from data covers the ground that matters. Where incursions happen, which fence lines are tested, which waterholes draw both animals and poachers — all of it is spatial. Recording patrols and incidents turns a ranger team from a presence into an evidence-gathering operation you can plan around.

Rangers we deploy log their patrols, and we feed that back to the landowner or NGO as coverage maps and incident reports rather than a verbal account at month end.
